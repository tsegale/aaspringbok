About the company

A small operation, run by someone who knows these routes.

Founder-led, based in Namibia, and built on 15 years of first-hand experience across the region's freight corridors.

Built on the road

Fifteen years of learning these routes first-hand.

AA Springbok Transport & Logistics CC was founded by Alies Asser Nowoseb, who has spent 15 years working in transport and logistics across the region. That time on the road shapes how the company plans a load, times a border crossing, and keeps a client's cargo moving instead of waiting in a queue.

The business is still run with that hands-on approach: a small, accountable operation rather than a call centre. There is no dispatch desk standing between the client and the person coordinating the load.
