Built on the road

Fifteen years of learning these routes the hard way.

AA Springbok Transport & Logistics CC was founded by Alies Asser Nowoseb, who has spent 15 years working in transport and logistics across the region. That time on the road shapes how the company plans a load, times a border crossing, and keeps a client's cargo moving instead of waiting in a queue.

The business is still run with that same hands-on approach today: a small, accountable operation rather than a call centre, so every job has someone who actually knows the route behind it. There is no dispatch desk standing between the client and the person driving, or coordinating, the load.
