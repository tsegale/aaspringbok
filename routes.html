How a route gets planned

Every corridor is planned around the border, not just the destination.

The route on paper is the easy part. What matters is the border post itself — its hours, its paperwork, and how it behaves on a given day.

Documentation prepared in advance

Cross-border paperwork sorted before the truck reaches the post, not while it's queued at the boom gate.

Timing built around border hours

Departure times set so trucks reach each crossing while it's actually open and moving.

Route knowledge, not a route planner app

Roads, detours, and seasonal conditions known first-hand from 15 years of driving them.
