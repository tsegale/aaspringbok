Built on the road

Led by someone who knows these routes.

The company was founded by Alies Asser Nowoseb, who has spent 15 years working in transport and logistics across the region. That time on the road shapes how each load is planned, how a border crossing is timed, and how cargo keeps moving instead of waiting in a queue.

It is run as a small, accountable operation rather than a call centre, so every job has someone behind it who knows the route.
